LocalOptimizationDoneRight.LocalOptimizationDoneRight — Module
LocalOptimizationDoneRight

The local-optimization evaluation engine of the ACCV 2026 paper "Score Sharp, Refine Soft, Polish Hard", reusable by its consumers (the paper project accv2026/ and the 3DV paper):

  • the per-pair pool-frame geometry (PairCtx, build_pair_ctx, model_imc) over VisualGeometryCore's light pool record PoolPair;
  • ONE refit layer for every local optimizer (refit_imc!, its extension point, and refit_model), with the evaluation-only LO strategies: PoseLib's truncated refiners (PoseLibLm, PoseLibLZ, PoseLibLmAdaptLossWidth) and the classical RansacLib LO baseline (LebedaIterLO, LebedaLOPlus, over the vendored lib/RansacLibLO);
  • post-LO error matrices of a selection grid (LoRefitSpec, apply_lo_refits) and the scene stream that feeds them (stream_selections);
  • the sample-budget engine (evaluate_sample_budget) and live end-to-end RANSAC on pool pairs (live_runs);
  • composed and solo arms, a paper's Roster of them, and the gate-enforced readers of a tuned operating point (deployed_budget_settings, audit_operating_points).

Pools are read with VisualGeometryEval (load_pool, scene_loaders); a consumer that selects (the budget engine, the stream) takes a pool's residual matrices too, every LO path only the light records.

RobustVisualGeometry.seed_partition_s2! — Method
seed_partition_s2!(ctx::PairCtx, seed_E, outlier_halfwidth::Float64) -> Union{Float64,Nothing}

The seed partition's squared scale ŝ² for a PairCtx, or nothing. This is the PairCtx method of RVG's seed_partition_s2! (its core method, in scoringglue.jl next to `loscales2, takes the workspace, problem, scoring and model); it builds the marginal atoutlierhalfwidthwith the ctx prior, fetches the cached per-ctx workspace, and delegates to that core method. Returnsnothing` on a partition too thin to carry a scale — the caller bails with no refit call ("did not travel" under onfail = NaN).

LocalOptimizationDoneRight.pair_chunk_budget — Function
pair_chunk_budget(M_max, T) -> Int

Pairs per chunk of a device selection grid over T columns of pools with up to M_max models. The chunk bounds the score block (Float64, pairs × M_max × T) plus argmax's broadcast temporary under ~6 GiB, clamped to [1, 256]. T-aware, so a wide miscalibration grid (T up to ~600) stays in device memory and narrow validation grids keep the chunk large.

LocalOptimizationDoneRight._poselib_loss_width_scale — Function
_poselib_loss_width_scale(g::PairGeom) -> Float64

The factor that converts a pool-frame loss width to a calibrated one. It is EXACTLY PoseLib's camera-aware convention (robust.cc: loss_scale · 0.5·(1/f₁ + 1/f₂), focal fᵢ = (fxᵢ+fyᵢ)/2 of the pool-frame intrinsics) — so the Lebeda arms and TLS-LM/LZ consume the same absolute pool-frame loss width on the E track.
